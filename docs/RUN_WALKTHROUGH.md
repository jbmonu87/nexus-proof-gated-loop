# A Real AI Work Loop: Review Changed the Deliverable

**October 1, 2026 showcase update · Account added October 2, 2026**

This walkthrough follows the public portfolio update that produced the Northstar model, memo, presentation, and Nexus captures. A separate reviewer found a financial-label error; the work was revised and reviewed again.

It is a bounded Codex-assisted knowledge-work example. It is not a run of Claude Primary's complete Nexus product-development state machine, and it does not prove autonomous in-app generation.

## The brief

I authorized the portfolio update, use of Nexus to create the captures, a synthetic showcase with no employer material, and a separate adversarial review for truthful claims and employment relevance.

The work needed to show both the product and my ability to work effectively with AI.

## What happened

| Moment | Observation or action | Consequence |

The initial and revised documents below let readers inspect the financial correction. This narrative records the review's finding; the complete agent conversations and internal traces are not published here.

## The judgment behind the correction

Both modeled scenarios hold revenue constant at $1,000,000 per month:

| Measure | Base | Cost downside |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Direct costs | $620,000 | $700,000 |
| Fixed costs | $260,000 | $260,000 |
| Modeled operating surplus | $120,000 | $40,000 |
| Surplus divided by revenue | 12% | 4% |

These are simplified synthetic scenarios before tax and financing. Deducting fixed costs makes “contribution margin” an inappropriate label for the resulting ratios. The arithmetic stayed the same; the interpretation and labels changed.

The separate reviewer identified that issue. I supplied the objective and constraints; I do not claim to have personally discovered the accounting error or performed every correction.
