# Case Study: Designing Accountable AI Work

I created Nexus to practice a demanding form of AI-enabled knowledge work: define a useful outcome, coordinate agents, make failure observable, and judge the result.

The project is a private, unshipped desktop workspace for business strategy. Its native PPTX, DOCX, and XLSX editors make it a useful test environment: a file can open while its interaction, content, layout, or saved state is still wrong.

## My contribution

I own product direction, operating design, acceptance standards, and final judgment. AI coding agents perform most implementation work. I turn broad needs into bounded work, design role and authority boundaries, and require evidence appropriate to the claim.

The primary orchestrator handles routine process decisions after approval. A bounded build orchestrator handles implementation. A different agent writes the gate when needed, and a different agent verifies the builder. The [execution graph](EXECUTION_GRAPH.md) makes stages, ownership, evidence, and recovery paths explicit.

This is the practical work behind my positioning as an AI workflow designer and product builder.

## What the evidence shows

The portfolio now pairs two development cases:

- A [failed interaction](AUDITABLE_RUN_CASE.md), where a correct target identity did not prove a usable click point.
- A [scoped persistence success](SUCCESSFUL_RUN_CASE.md), where independent checks passed and deleting the reader repair made the relevant checks fail again.

Both are useful. One shows willingness to reject inadequate evidence; the other shows a test that responds to the actual repair.

## Connecting the method to business work

The [Northstar work sample](BUSINESS_WORKFLOW_CASE.md) links assumptions and calculations to a recommendation, then to a memo and presentation. The model's cost downside is deliberately narrow. The memo rejects the unsupported claim that the proposal stays profitable under every downside.

That is the judgment I want to bring to a team: frame the decision, make assumptions visible, coordinate production, challenge unsupported conclusions, and give the decision-maker a clear deliverable.

Northstar is synthetic and was constructed for the portfolio with AI assistance. It is not evidence of a client engagement, measured business impact, or autonomous generation inside Nexus.

## What remains to prove

Nexus has no public release or production customers. Export, preservation, fidelity, and editing coverage remain incomplete. The new demo itself surfaced spreadsheet formatting-persistence limits, recorded in [current status](CURRENT_STATUS.md).

This portfolio demonstrates a working practice and a substantial prototype. It does not claim that I personally wrote all the code or that the product is ready for enterprise deployment.
