# Nexus: Strategy to Execution With AI

**Strategic problem solving · Practical AI fluency · Agentic orchestration**

I’m JB Monu, a strategy and operations leader. I help leaders turn ambiguous priorities into clear decisions, workable operating approaches, and useful new capabilities.

My background spans strategy consulting and cross-functional transformation. The work I do best is framing the real problem, evaluating the options, aligning people with different priorities, and helping move the work into implementation or a useful handoff.

Nexus is where I extend that practice with hands-on AI experimentation. It is a private R&D project: a local-first desktop workspace for business strategy, with native presentation, document, and spreadsheet editors.

## My role in Nexus

I own product direction, workflow design, acceptance standards, and final judgment. AI coding agents perform most implementation work. After I approve an outcome, the primary orchestrator handles routine routing and sequencing; a bounded build orchestrator handles implementation and retries.

I made preservation a product requirement: content Nexus cannot render must still survive save. An editor's support limit is not permission to discard a user's work. Complete preservation has not yet been demonstrated.

[Read how Nexus connects to my broader work](docs/CASE_STUDY.md).

## One visible loop: review changed the deliverable

A separate reviewer caught an incorrect financial label in the first showcase. The arithmetic deducted fixed costs, so “contribution margin” was the wrong term. The inputs and real Nexus captures were revised to use a defined operating-surplus measure.

That is a small, concrete example of the work I want AI to help with: produce something useful, inspect it, and revise it when the evidence warrants a change.
